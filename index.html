ABOUT AUREUM

We work where land meets possibility.

Aureum Industries is a strategic land, development and transaction platform focused on unlocking complex opportunities across Maharashtra and beyond.

From identifying land to facilitating acquisition, development and strategic transactions, we bring together commercial understanding, local intelligence and execution capability.

Our role is simple: understand the opportunity, navigate the complexity and move the transaction forward.
